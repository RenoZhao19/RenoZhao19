# Hi, I’m Reno Zhao

I’m an EECS undergraduate at UC Berkeley interested in robot learning and control.

## Current project · FACTR 2 on Hexfellow

My team and I are working to reproduce FACTR 2’s force-feedback teleoperation experiments on the Hexfellow MAVER X4. We’re starting with NEXT, the paper’s method for estimating how much torque contact adds at each joint. The goal is to feel through the controller when Hexfellow’s arm meets resistance. [FACTR 2 paper and demos](https://jasonjzliu.com/factr2/)
